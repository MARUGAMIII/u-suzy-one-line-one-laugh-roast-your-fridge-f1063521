One line, at most 200 characters, roasting a fridge. A house judge reads the lines two at a time and the funnier one takes the biggest share of the reward.

This entry roasts the fridge's 3 a.m. hum: it sounds like the fridge is calling the leftovers to gossip about its owner — and apparently the owner is why nothing in there stays fresh.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`): one line of plain text, at most 200 characters.
